The raw results of these campaigns stayed under `/aisi-vepfs/outisli/gpu_port`, untracked, until the deployment of section 9 and were removed with it; the tables of this file are their record.

## 2. Environment of the node

### 2.1 Library path: a deployment requirement

The container's environment sets `LD_LIBRARY_PATH=/usr/local/nccl-rdma-sharp-plugins/lib:/usr/local/cuda/lib64` (from the ML platform, through sshd; no rc file sets it). The loader searches `LD_LIBRARY_PATH` before the RUNPATH of torch's CUDA 12.9 wheels, so a process that does not start in Python, LAMMPS among them, binds the 12.8 toolkit's `libnvJitLink` and `libcudart`, and the DeePMD-kit PyTorch backend then fails to load:

```
Unable to load PyTorch Exportable backend plugin (libdeepmd_backend_ptexpt.so) ...
libcusparse.so.12: undefined symbol: __nvJitLinkGetErrorLogSize_12_9, version libnvJitLink.so.12
```

`lmp -h` does not load the backend, so it does not show the failure; a run with a model does. Every process that runs DeePMD-kit on this node therefore needs the wheel's CUDA library directories ahead of the toolkit:

- The harness does it in `machine.sh`, whenever torch's CUDA version is newer than the toolkit's.
- The user's shell does it through two activation hooks of `dpmd-new`: `etc/conda/activate.d/lammps-outisli-cuda.sh` puts the 15 directories `site-packages/nvidia/*/lib` first on `LD_LIBRARY_PATH` and `lammps-outisli/bin` on `PATH`, and `etc/conda/deactivate.d/lammps-outisli-cuda.sh` removes exactly those entries again. conda exports its own `PATH` before it runs a deactivation hook and, on a reactivation, after it, so the hooks edit the lists in place and add nothing that is already present. With `conda activate dpmd-new` alone, `lmp -k on g 1 -sf kk` runs a model, and its run-0 dump equals that of the harness bit for bit; `conda deactivate` restores both variables byte for byte.

### 2.2 The profiler loses its kernel records late in a long process

In a long pytest process, torch.profiler (CUPTI 12.9 of the torch wheels on the 535 driver) can stop recording GPU activity while it goes on recording the runtime calls: a profiled region then holds `cudaLaunchKernel` and `cudaLaunchKernelExC` but no kernel and no memcpy record. After `test_dpa4c_cuda.py` in the same process, `test_fused_network_matches_the_layer_kernels_bitwise` therefore saw no `fused_network_kernel` in its traces and reported 26 of its 32 networks as skipped, although the fused network kept launching; alone, the test passes all 256 subtests. The loss needs several tests together: the tests from `test_pair_potential_matches_the_portable_term[...nlh]` to the end of `test_dpa4c_cuda.py` cause it, each quarter of the second half of the suite alone does not, 3,000 profiler sessions of one kernel do not, and neither do 2,000,000 kernel records in 400 sessions. The RTX PRO 6000 (CUDA 13.3) does not show it. In the merged code no test depends on these records in a long process: the fused-network test reads the route of each run from the dispatch rule of the operator (`graph_fitting_route_rule`) instead of a trace, and the DPA4C trace checks run in fresh subprocesses. Every profiled process on this node is kept short and its report checked for kernel records before use.

### 2.3 Eager module loading is unusable

With `CUDA_MODULE_LOADING=EAGER`, even `torch.randn(10, device="cuda")` fails with `cudaErrorSymbolNotFound` on this node: by inference, the 535 driver cannot load some module of the torch cu129 libraries, which lazy loading, the default, never loads.

The fused network wins at the widths of 96 and 192 and loses at 256, also after twenty waves of panels (100,800 atoms), so wave quantization alone does not explain it. At 10,296 nodes the fused network of width 256 runs at 13.5 TFLOP/s, 34% of the 39.5 TFLOP/s fp32 rate, and the per-layer kernels at about 15.6 TFLOP/s for that width and 18.4 TFLOP/s (47%) for the width of 384 of Plus. Inference: the per-layer kernels compute 64 × 128 tiles, which waste a quarter of their columns at the widths of 96 and 192 and none at 256 and 384, and on a device whose fp32 ridge is about 10 FLOP/B the activation traffic that the fusion saves is worth less than the arithmetic rate it gives up. The rule is led by the L20 engineer (fp32 ridge of `dpa4_fp32_ridge`, possibly with the width).

### 4.4 No fused shape for the width of 384

The fused network serves a width of 384 only up to one wave of panels; beyond it the per-layer kernels run, which take 1.127 of the 2.977 ms of the Plus step at 10,296 atoms (38%). The limit on this device is the register file, not the shared memory: the single-CTA shape `FusedShape<384, 1, 8, 4, 1, 12, 1, 3, 8>` holds 94,848 bytes, two of which would fit the 228 KB of a multiprocessor, but it takes 163 registers for each of its 384 threads, 62,592 of the 65,536 registers, so a multiprocessor holds one CTA; a 64-node panel of that width would take 146 KB and run one CTA per multiprocessor as well. Such a shape would have to beat the 47% of the fp32 rate of the per-layer kernels, while the fused network reaches 34% at a width of 256 on this device, so no throughput shape for the width of 384 is built.

### 4.5 Bond-vector prefetch

`BOND_PREFETCH=1` shortens the model step at 10,296 atoms by 0.3% to 1.2% (section 3.3), where the 35 MB of bond vectors fit the 60 MB L2 and the derived choice keeps it off; below the 3% bar, the derived rule stands.

## 5. Register budgets of the edge kernels

## 6. Fused fitting rules

The rules of 1618f27fd (from the L20 engineer: the fp32 ridge of the device against width / 4, and the 64-node panels of the width of 96 where they need fewer waves) route the width of 256 to the per-layer kernels at every run of more panels than multiprocessors on this device, whose ridge is 10 FLOP/B, and leave the other widths and the latency and cluster shapes as they were (profiler traces at 512, 2,048, 10,296 and 100,800 nodes). Their outputs are bit for bit those of the previous build (`bench_ops.py` at three sizes, the 30 LAMMPS dumps), and in LAMMPS they shorten the step of Neo by 5.2% and 2.1% on carbon and 4.9% and 3.9% on BCN at 10,296 and 100,800 atoms, and of Air by 4.0% and 1.4% on carbon and 3.6% and 1.4% on BCN; Nano and Mini stay within 0.4%. At the width of 96, the 64-node panels take the step of Nano at 10,296 atoms 1.3% longer (both shapes need two waves there, and the rule keeps the 32-node panels) and leave it unchanged at 17,280 atoms, where they need two waves against three.

### 9.1 MPI

The system Open MPI 4.1.2 (`/usr/bin/mpicxx`) is not CUDA-aware (`ompi_info --parsable --all`: `opal_built_with_cuda_support:value:false`). The fork, the C++ interface and its plugin, and the Python package are therefore built against Open MPI 5.0.10 with UCX 1.20.1 of conda-forge in the environment `openmpi` (`/aisi-vepfs/outisli/miniforge3/envs/openmpi`; builds h67ed482_1 and hbe80e26_0, those of the workstation), which is built with CUDA support (`opal_built_with_cuda_support:value:true`) and whose UCX has the `cuda_copy` and `cuda_ipc` transports. Its `etc/openmpi-mca-params.conf` turns the CUDA support off (`opal_cuda_support = 0`). A launch of several ranks, which exchange device buffers, turns it on together with the memory-type query of UCX, `mpirun --mca opal_cuda_support 1 -x UCX_MEMTYPE_CACHE=n`, as the multi-rank scans of the harness and `step8_mgpu.sh` do. A single rank leaves it off: the support adds a memory-type query to every MPI call, and a rank without peers exchanges no device buffer (section 9.5 gives the effect on this node). The machine profile `vepfs.sh` therefore exports neither variable, and the runtime environment of section 9.7 sets `UCX_MEMTYPE_CACHE=n` only.

Every `mpirun` of that environment crashed on this VM with signal 11, `mpirun -np 1 hostname` included. gdb places the crash in hwloc 2.13.0, in `hwloc_bitmap_snprintf` under the XML export of the topology, which `pmix_hwloc_setup_topology` calls from `PMIx_server_init` in `prte_init` (PRRTE 3.0.13). `lstopo --of xml` of the same environment crashes alike; it succeeds with `HWLOC_COMPONENTS=-linuxio` or with `--no-io`, and still crashes with `-pci`, with the GPU components switched off or with `--filter osdev:none`. By inference, the Linux I/O discovery of hwloc meets the unusual topology of the VM, whose second socket has no Package object and whose extra Group object holds two host bridges, and leaves an object whose set the export cannot print; no upstream report was found, and the conda package pins libhwloc to 2.13.0. `HWLOC_COMPONENTS=-linuxio` costs hwloc only the network and disk OS devices, and with it every `mpirun` works. The harness takes the value from the machine profile (`BENCH_HWLOC_COMPONENTS`, which `vepfs.sh` sets to `-cuda,-nvml,-gl,-opencl,-rsmi,-linuxio`), and the runtime environment sets it.

The C++ interface built before the deployment linked no MPI: the test program of CMake's FindMPI did not link (`MPI_RESULT_CXX_test_mpi_normal` false), because the linker of `dpmd-new` did not find the libraries that the system `libmpi.so` depends on (`libopen-rte`, `libopen-pal` and `libhwloc` under `/usr/lib/x86_64-linux-gnu`), so its `libdeepmd_op_pt.so` was compiled without `USE_MPI`, while the operator library of the Python package linked the system Open MPI through g++ 11.4. With the conda Open MPI the FindMPI tests of both builds pass, and `libmpi.so.40` of that environment is the only MPI library of the node's DeePMD-kit and LAMMPS: the operator libraries of the package and of the prefix and `liblammps.so` of the fork need it, and the plugin, as designed, uses the one of LAMMPS.

The route of each run size is measured when a model is frozen (`deepmd/pt_expt/kernels/fitting_routes.py`, section 8). The rule of the last freeze of this section is that of the commit `fix(dpa4c): check the fitting routes on trains of two node compositions` (`e892bbbe6`); section 9.10 gives the rules that replaced it. The screen times the routes in alternating short bursts on a periodic bulk system of three node types drawn among the first three of the model. A route other than that of the rules wins the screen only by a median gain over the rules above the larger of 1% and the half-range of the per-capture gains, on at least three captures. Such a route takes the run size only if it also wins on trains of one route at a time, in which each route replays alone for at least 500 ms of device time before its timed replays. The trains run on two compositions of the sample, first with every node of type 0 and then with its three node types, each in two captures that time the two routes in opposite orders. The route needs a positive median gain on both compositions, and the check stops at the first on which it does not. Between two routes of which neither is that of the rules, which the bisection of a band compares, the trains alone decide, by the mean of the median gains of the two compositions. The trains exist because a board at its power limit clocks a route by the power that the route draws, so that a route that wins in bursts can lose in a deployment that keeps the device busy; the commit message measures this on an L20 at its 350 W limit, where the per-layer kernels of a native-spin Air model at 129,536 nodes lose 4% to 6% on trains of one node type. ### 9.7 Runtime environment

The environment `dpmd-new` carries a pair of conda hooks, `etc/conda/activate.d/lammps-outisli-cuda.sh` and `deactivate.d/lammps-outisli-cuda.sh`, that make a shell of this node run the formal install: the 15 CUDA library directories of the torch wheel (`site-packages/nvidia/*/lib`) lead `LD_LIBRARY_PATH`, the bin directories of the Open MPI environment and of the fork lead `PATH`, and `LAMMPS_PLUGIN_PATH` (the plugin directory of the C++ prefix), `UCX_MEMTYPE_CACHE=n` and `HWLOC_COMPONENTS=-linuxio` are set. The wheel directories have to lead because the container's `LD_LIBRARY_PATH` names the CUDA 12.8 toolkit (`/usr/local/cuda/lib64`) and the NCCL plugins, the dynamic loader searches `LD_LIBRARY_PATH` before the RUNPATH of the wheel libraries, and a process that does not start in Python, LAMMPS among them, would bind the older `libnvJitLink` and `libcudart` of the toolkit, on which the PyTorch backend of DeePMD-kit fails to load (undefined symbol `__nvJitLinkGetErrorLogSize_12_9`).

The hooks do not put the library directory of the C++ prefix on `LD_LIBRARY_PATH`, since Python would then bind the libraries of the prefix instead of those of the package; the plugin finds them through its RUNPATH (section 9.2). They leave `OMPI_MCA_opal_cuda_support` unset, because the launch of several ranks turns the CUDA support of Open MPI on (section 9.1); as root, `mpirun` needs `OMPI_ALLOW_RUN_AS_ROOT=1` and `OMPI_ALLOW_RUN_AS_ROOT_CONFIRM=1`, which the machine profile `vepfs.sh` exports. A reactivation adds nothing twice, and the deactivation removes exactly the entries that the activation added and restores the three variables to their earlier values or unsets them. Placing `openmpi/bin` first on `PATH` also shadows the `openssl`, `c_rehash` and ICU tools of conda-forge in `dpmd-new`, which no part of the install uses.

`env_check.sh` activates the environment in an interactive login shell and checks which `lmp`, `mpirun` and `python` resolve, the variables, the plugin list, a run of two ranks with the launch of section 9.1, the libraries that run binds, the reactivation and the restoration. In zsh with conda, zsh with mamba and bash with conda alike, `lmp` is the `bin/lmp` of the fork and `mpirun` the Open MPI 5.0.10 of the environment `openmpi`; the plugin lists its 13 styles without an open error; `OMPI_MCA_opal_cuda_support` is unset and the other three variables are set; the run of two ranks (Nano at 10,296 atoms on GPUs 0 and 1) ends with return code 0 and without the warning that LAMMPS turns GPU-aware MPI off, loads the DeePMD-kit libraries of both ranks from `deepmd-kit_cpp/lib` and `libmpi.so.40` from the environment `openmpi`, and gives the same step-0 thermo row and byte for byte the same dump in the three shells; the reactivation leaves one entry of each kind on `PATH` and the 17 entries of `LD_LIBRARY_PATH` that one activation sets (the 15 wheel directories and the two inherited ones, the NCCL plugins and the CUDA toolkit), and the deactivation restores `PATH`, `LD_LIBRARY_PATH` and the variables exactly.

zsh initializes conda and mamba, bash conda alone: its `~/.bashrc` holds no mamba block. In a fresh zsh, the first `mamba activate X; mamba deactivate` moves `miniforge3/bin` to the front of `PATH` for any environment X, the `openmpi` environment, which has no hooks, included (`mamba_control.sh`), so `env_check.sh` makes one such round trip before it takes the snapshot that the deactivation has to restore.

### 9.8 Concurrent runs on one node

The `lammps` stage of `device_suite.sh` runs four single-GPU LAMMPS processes at the same time, one per GPU, each of them one run after the other. A run of five-element Nano at 100,800 atoms lasts 4.4 s, of which the timed run of 200 steps takes 1.1 s, so the timed run of a process overlaps the start or the end of another one unless the four are in phase. The point reads 5.484 ms in the final campaign (range of the six runs 2.3%, fastest run 5.449 ms) and 5.922 ms in the campaign of section 9.5 (12.2%, fastest run 5.429 ms; 5.787 ms, 14.0% and 5.390 ms in its first run), and it is the one point of the 75 that is slower than the final campaign by more than the run range of that campaign (+8.0%). The cause is the start of other processes on the node.

The archive is not the cause: on the frozen geometry (`in_frozen.lammps`, GPU 0 alone, four alternating rounds of 1,000 steps) the archive of the final campaign takes 3.4153 ms (3.4146 to 3.4308) and that of the first freeze of the deployment 3.4142 ms (3.4137 to 3.4151), −0.03%. ### 9.10 Final rule of the route tuner

Two commits followed the freezes of section 9.5. The second, `f5f9e6a39`, is the final tuner, and `outisli` stands at it. The first, `fix(dpa4c): hold the fitting routes to the tie on trains` (`867480bd1`), changes the check on trains. Every route whose median gain over the rules in the alternating replays exceeds its bound, the larger of 1% and the half-range of its per-capture gains, is a candidate. The candidates are checked on trains of one route at a time in the order of their gains, and the first that holds takes the run size; where none holds, the rules keep it. A candidate holds where its median gain over the rules on the trains is at least −1%, the tie, on every composition: first with every node of type 0, then with the node types of the sample, and the check of a candidate stops at the first composition below −1%. A model of one node type trains one composition, since its two compositions coincide, and the bisection of a band, which compares the two routes besides the rules, decides as before on the mean of the median gains of the two compositions. The commit message gives the reason: on a V100 at its 300 W limit, the per-layer kernels of a trained Mini model at 10,296 nodes gain 0.3% to 1.1% on trains, which the earlier check refused in some freezes, although they shorten the LAMMPS step at 10,296 atoms by 1.9% to 2.7% on carbon, BCN and five elements, while losses of up to 12% on an L20 at its power limit stay refused.

The second, `fix(dpa4c): settle every fitting route before a sample stops` (`f5f9e6a39`), changes when a sample stops taking alternating captures. A sample takes further captures while a gain lies near its bound, and it used to stop once the route of the largest gain was settled, so that a runner-up within the margin of its own bound could depart from the rules on three captures where seven would have left the run size to them. It now stops only where the median gain of every route besides the rules lies beyond the margin of its stage. The check on trains is unchanged.
